Our Story

Founded on a love for pets and a passion for design.

At Pawsh Fashion, we believe that our pets are not just companions?”they are family. And family deserves the absolute best. We started our journey in 2026 with a simple mission: to bridge the gap between high-end fashion and pet lifestyle.


Every piece in our collection is meticulously curated for quality, comfort, and style. From our hand-knitted sweaters to our interactive smart toys, we prioritize materials that are safe for your pets and designs that look beautiful in your home.


Thank you for letting us be a part of your pet's journey. We are honored to dress the world's most stylish paws.
